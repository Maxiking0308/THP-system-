Recruitment & Applications

Apply to become a Texas Highway Patrol RP Trooper and start your law enforcement career path.

Requirements

  • Minimum age: 16+ (server policy configurable)
  • Working microphone and stable internet connection
  • Fluent in English and able to follow verbal instructions
  • Basic understanding of GTA Roleplay rules and etiquette
  • Ability to attend scheduled trainings and patrols
  • No recent serious bans on the community server

Process

  1. Submit the application form below.
  2. Wait for review by Recruitment and Training staff.
  3. Complete an interview on Discord or in-game.
  4. Attend Basic Trooper Training if accepted.
